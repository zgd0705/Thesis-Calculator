Method notes

Each lane profile is the mean signal across the lane width, sampled along the lane's own centre line from well to dye front, so slanted and smiling lanes are measured correctly.

Ladder bands are matched to the known sizes from the top (largest) down. The standard curve regresses log₁₀(size) on Rf. Sizes outside the ladder's Rf range are extrapolated and marked with “~”.

Integrated area is the baseline-corrected profile summed between band boundaries. Boundaries are where the signal falls to 3% of the peak, or the valley between two neighbouring bands. It is a relative quantity, in intensity × px.
